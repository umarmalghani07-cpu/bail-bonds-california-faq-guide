Bail Bonds in Inglewood, California — 2026 Guide

Inglewood is a city of approximately 110,000 in the southwest Los Angeles County area, served by the Inglewood Police Department (IPD). Arrests in Inglewood are booked at the IPD facility before potential transfer to the Los Angeles County Jail system.

Arrest and Booking Process in Inglewood

Inglewood Police Department Jail:

  • Address: 1 Manchester Blvd, Inglewood, CA 90301
  • Phone: (310) 412-5210

When someone is arrested in Inglewood, they are taken to the IPD jail for booking. This includes fingerprinting, photographing, and entering charges into the system. For misdemeanors, the defendant may be released from IPD directly. For felonies, transfer to an LA County Sheriff facility typically follows.

Transfer timing: Transfers to county facilities usually occur within 24–48 hours of booking, though misdemeanor defendants may be released before any transfer occurs.

Bail Amounts in Inglewood

Inglewood arrests follow the Los Angeles County bail schedule. Bail amounts for common charges:

Charge Typical Bail
DUI (first offense, misdemeanor) $5,000
Assault and battery $20,000
Drug possession $10,000–$25,000
Residential burglary $50,000
Robbery $100,000
Weapons (loaded firearm) $25,000

These are preset amounts based on the charge type. A judge may increase or decrease bail at arraignment based on the defendant's criminal history, flight risk, and public safety considerations under California Penal Code § 1275.

Finding Someone Booked in Inglewood

  1. At IPD: Call (310) 412-5210 (non-emergency line) and ask for the jail unit
  2. After county transfer: Search the LA County Sheriff Inmate Information Center

Knowing which facility currently holds the defendant is critical before a bondsman can post. A bail bond agent serving Inglewood will confirm the location for you.

Inglewood Jail Processing Times

The IPD jail is a smaller facility compared to the county system. This generally means:

  • Faster processing for misdemeanor releases (2–6 hours)
  • Predictable workflow — IPD staff know the local bondsmen and process bonds efficiently
  • Weekend availability — IPD processes releases 24/7

After county transfer, processing reverts to LA County timelines (6–18 hours at Men's Central Jail or IRC).

Nearby Inglewood — SoFi Stadium Area Arrests

With SoFi Stadium hosting NFL games, concerts, and major events, Inglewood sees event-related arrests that can include public intoxication, assault, and trespassing. These are typically misdemeanor charges covered by the preset bail schedule, and same-day release is often achievable with a licensed bondsman on call.

Posting Bail for an Inglewood Arrest

  1. Confirm the defendant's location (IPD vs. transferred to county)
  2. Contact a licensed LA County bondsman
  3. Provide the booking number, charges, and bail amount
  4. Sign the indemnity agreement and pay the 10% premium
  5. The bondsman posts at IPD or the applicable LA County facility

Bail bond services in Inglewood are available 24/7 with mobile agents familiar with both IPD and the LA County transfer process.
